DATA PROTECTION

Privacy & responsible use

SchoolPass is designed for authorized school administration use. The school remains responsible for its official data-protection decisions and notices.

What the system stores

  • Student identity and school enrollment information entered by the school.
  • Student photo when the school chooses to upload one.
  • Arrival records containing date, time, student reference and scanner device.
  • Fee and uniform status restricted to authorized finance/admin users.
  • Audit records of important administrative operations.

Access controls

Student and arrival functions require authentication. Finance pages require finance/admin roles. Administrative actions such as imports and card replacement require the administrator role.

QR design

The QR credential is an opaque random identifier. It is not a container for a student's name, fees or other personal information.

Before production

The school should complete its controller/processor responsibilities, user authorization, retention schedule, backup policy, incident process and any required registration/assessment with the relevant Rwandan authority.
